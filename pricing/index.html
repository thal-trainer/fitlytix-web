Questions

About pricing

Can we try it first?
Yes. We walk you through a demo gym we have already set up, so you can see every screen working with realistic data before deciding anything. If you want to go ahead, we then create your own gym. Book a demo to set that up.
What counts as a trainer account?
Anyone who logs in to manage clients, so trainers and gym admins. Client accounts are unlimited on every plan and are never counted.
What if we grow past our plan?
Move up whenever you need to. We charge you the difference for the rest of the period and nothing about your data changes.
Can we cancel?
Yes, at the end of any billing period. Ask for your export first. See the terms.
How do we pay?
We are still finalising payment options. Ask us on your demo call and we will tell you exactly where this stands.
