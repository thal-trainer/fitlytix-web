The problem

Sound familiar?

"Everything my trainer knows is in his notebook." And it leaves when he does.

"Plans are spread across multiple communication channels." Nobody can find anything.

Fitlytix puts all of it in one place. Client records, plans, sessions and progress live with your gym, not in someone's phone.

What you get

Built for how gyms actually work

Sessions and attendance

Workout plans

Build a plan once and give it to as many clients as you want. Injuries show on the profile so nothing gets missed.

Progress tracking

Goals, health details, contact numbers and full history in one profile. Any trainer can pick up where another left off.

Questions

Before you book

Does it work on any phone?
Yes. Fitlytix runs on Android and iPhone. Your trainers do not need a computer and there is no equipment to buy.
Are my trainers technical enough for this?
If they can use WhatsApp, they can use Fitlytix. Most trainers are comfortable after one short session, and we run that session for you.
Do we have to sign a long contract?
No. You pay monthly and can stop at the end of any billing period. Annual billing is available if you prefer it.
What if we need help?
Email us or message us on WhatsApp. We reply within two working days.
What happens to our data if we leave?
It is yours. Ask for an export at any time and we will send you everything. We do not hold gym records hostage.
How long does setup take?
Most gyms are running in two days. We set your trainers up on day one, and you add your clients on day two.
