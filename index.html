What FITLYTIX does

Everything a trainer needs

Built from the ground up for gym trainers and owners. No bloat, no fluff - just the tools that matter.

Client Management

Full client profiles with health metrics, goals, fitness level, injuries and contact details. Everything in one place.

Workout Builder

Build multi-day workout programs with exercises from a library of 30+ movements. Assign directly to clients.

Progress Tracking

Log body metrics, track BMI, and visualise client progress over time with built-in charts.

Nutrition Plans

Build meal plans with macro tracking. Assign active plans to clients and monitor their nutrition goals.

Schedule

Weekly calendar view of all sessions. Mark sessions as completed, missed or cancelled. See today's sessions at a glance.

Admin Panel

Gym owners get a full admin dashboard. Invite trainers, manage clients across the gym, and track performance.
