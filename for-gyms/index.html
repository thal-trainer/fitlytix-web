Questions

What owners ask us

My trainers are not technical. Will they use it?
If they can use WhatsApp, they can use this. The daily job is open the app, see today, tap through the workout. We train your team in one short session.
We already keep everything in a spreadsheet.
A spreadsheet cannot tell a trainer what a client is doing today, show a member their own progress, or stop a departing trainer walking off with the only copy. Fitlytix puts the same records in your trainers' pockets, keeps the full history with the gym, and gives every client their plan without anyone having to send it.
What happens when a trainer leaves?
Remove their access from the admin panel. It stops straight away. Their clients stay with the gym and you move them to another trainer without losing any history.
Do we have to move everyone at once?
No. Most gyms start with trainers and a handful of clients, then add the rest over a few weeks. Nothing stops working while you move across.
What if we want to leave later?
Ask for an export and we send you everything. Your records are yours. See the terms.
